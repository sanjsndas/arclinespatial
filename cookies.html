    1. What are cookies?

    Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, or other device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, to remember your preferences, and to provide information to the website owners about how their site is being used.

    Cookies do not typically contain any information that personally identifies a user, but personal information that we store about you may be linked to the information stored in and obtained from cookies. For more information about how we handle personal data, please see our Privacy Policy.

    In addition to cookies, we may use other similar technologies such as web beacons (also known as pixel tags or clear GIFs) and local storage. This policy covers all such technologies, though we refer to them collectively as "cookies" for simplicity.

    2. How we use cookies

    We use cookies for the following general purposes:

    • To make our website work. Some cookies are essential for the basic functioning of our site — for example, to remember your cookie preferences or to enable secure areas of the site.
    • To remember your preferences. Cookies help us remember choices you make, such as your preferred language or region, so you don't have to re-enter them each time you visit.
    • To analyse and improve our site. We use analytics cookies to understand how visitors interact with our content — which articles are most read, how visitors navigate between pages, and where we can improve the experience. This data is aggregated and anonymised wherever possible.
    • To measure the effectiveness of our content. We may use cookies to understand which of our articles and features are most valuable to our readers.

    We do not use cookies to serve targeted advertising, and we do not sell cookie data to third parties for marketing purposes.

    3. Types of cookies we use

    The table below explains the specific categories of cookies we use, what they do, and how long they last.

    Category Purpose Duration
    Essential Required for the website to function properly. These enable core features such as page navigation, secure areas, and remembering your cookie consent choice. The website cannot function without these cookies. Session to 12 months
    Preference Remember choices you make — such as your language, region, or display preferences — to provide a more personalised experience. Up to 12 months
    Analytics Collect aggregated, anonymised information about how visitors use our site — pages visited, time spent, and navigation paths. This helps us understand and improve our content. Up to 26 months
    Performance Help us monitor the technical performance of our site, such as page load times and any errors, so we can ensure a reliable experience. Session to 12 months

    First-party vs. third-party cookies

    First-party cookies are set by Arcline Spatial Design directly. Third-party cookies are set by a domain other than ours — typically by service providers we use, such as analytics platforms. We carefully select our third-party providers and require them to protect your data appropriately.

    4. Third-party cookies

    We use a limited number of trusted third-party services that may set cookies on your device when you visit our website. These include:

    • Analytics providers. We use privacy-focused analytics to understand site usage. Where possible, we configure these services to anonymise IP addresses and avoid tracking individuals across other websites.
    • Email service providers. If you subscribe to our newsletter, our email delivery provider may use cookies or similar technologies to track whether emails have been opened and links clicked. This helps us understand the effectiveness of our communications.
    • Embedded content. Some of our articles may include embedded content (such as videos or social media posts). These third-party platforms may set their own cookies when you interact with that content.

    We do not control third-party cookies. You should review the privacy and cookie policies of the relevant third parties for more information about how they use cookies.

    5. Managing your cookie preferences

    When you first visit our website, you will be shown a cookie banner where you can accept or customise your cookie preferences. You can change your preferences at any time by clicking the cookie settings link in the footer or by clearing your browser cookies and reloading the page.

    You can choose to accept all cookies, accept only essential cookies, or customise which categories you allow. Please note that blocking certain cookies may affect the functionality and performance of our website.

    6. Browser controls

    Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can typically:

    • View the cookies stored on your device and delete them individually or in bulk.
    • Block third-party cookies while allowing first-party cookies.
    • Block all cookies from specific sites or from all sites.
    • Delete all cookies when you close your browser.
    • Browse in private or incognito mode, which limits the cookies stored on your device.

    The exact steps for managing cookies depend on your browser. Here are links to the cookie management help pages for the most common browsers:

    • Google Chrome
    • Mozilla Firefox
    • Safari
    • Microsoft Edge

    If you use a different browser, please consult its help documentation for cookie management instructions.

    7. Do Not Track signals

    Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that sends a signal to websites indicating that you do not wish to be tracked. There is currently no universal standard for how websites should respond to DNT signals, and our website does not currently respond to them.

    However, you can control tracking through the cookie preferences described in Section 5 and through your browser settings as described in Section 6.

When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you through a notice on our website or by email.

    We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies.
